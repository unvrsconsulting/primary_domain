AI Automation for Dental Practices

Front desk overload, no-shows, and a recall list that never gets worked. These are administrative problems, and they have an administrative fix.

1 in 3US dental practices reported using at least one AI tool as of 2026, up sharply from a few years ago

A dental front desk juggles phones, check-ins, insurance questions, and scheduling all at once, which means callers wait on hold and the recall list (patients overdue for a cleaning) rarely gets worked consistently. Every one of those overdue patients is revenue sitting on the table.

Everything we build for dental practices handles scheduling, reminders, and administrative communication only. It never touches clinical records or diagnosis, that stays entirely with your clinical team and your existing HIPAA-compliant systems.

Why this works for dental practices

  • An AI receptionist can answer routine scheduling and insurance-hours questions without pulling staff away from patients in the chair.
  • Recall and reactivation (texting patients overdue for a cleaning) is one of the highest-ROI, most-neglected automations in a dental practice.
  • Appointment reminders sent automatically cut no-shows, which directly protects chair-time revenue.

Beyond the standard setup for dental practices

1

Recall campaigns segmented by treatment

Patients overdue for a cleaning versus a follow-up procedure get different, more relevant outreach instead of one generic reminder.

2

Insurance verification pre-visit

Basic insurance and eligibility details get collected and checked before the patient arrives, cutting front-desk time per visit.

3

No-show risk scoring

Patients with a pattern of no-shows get an extra reminder or a confirmation call, while reliable patients aren't over-messaged.
