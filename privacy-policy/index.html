Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

UNVRS ("we," "us," "our") operates universeaiconsulting.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide directly. When you submit the contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, website, and whatever you tell us about what you'd like to automate.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies (via Google Tag Manager and standard analytics tools) to understand how visitors use the Site, including pages viewed, general location, device and browser type, and referring site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about our services.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site.
  • To understand aggregate traffic and usage patterns.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How your information is shared

Contact form submissions are delivered to us by email via MailChannels, a transactional email provider. Site analytics run through Google Tag Manager, which may use cookies subject to Google's own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Cookies

The Site uses cookies to support basic functionality and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may not function as intended without them.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, and delete or anonymize them when no longer needed.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you've submitted to us by emailing connor@universeaiconsulting.com.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email connor@universeaiconsulting.com.
