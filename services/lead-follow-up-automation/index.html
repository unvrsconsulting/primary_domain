AI Lead Follow-Up Automation

A lead contacted within five minutes converts dramatically better than one contacted an hour later. This system makes sure five minutes never turns into five hours.

A new lead comes in through a form, a DM, or a missed call, and the clock starts running. Most small businesses lose a meaningful share of leads simply because nobody responded fast enough, not because the lead wasn't interested.

This automation removes the dependency on a person remembering to follow up. The moment a lead comes in, they get a personal-feeling text or email within seconds. If they don't respond, the system keeps following up on a schedule, same day, next day, a few days later, until they book or opt out.

What it does

  • Texts and emails a new lead within seconds of form submission, DM, or missed call
  • Keeps following up on a set schedule until the lead responds or books
  • Routes hot leads straight to your phone or CRM inbox
  • Tracks response time and close rate by lead source
  • Works across web forms, Instagram/Facebook DMs, and missed calls
  • Hands off to your AI receptionist or a real person once the lead is ready to book
