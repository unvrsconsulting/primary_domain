Document & Chat AI

Custom AI Chatbot & Auto-Bot

Most chatbots read from a script. This one is trained on your actual business and can read a document, pull the right fields, and take the next step correctly.

Generic chatbot widgets frustrate customers because they can't handle anything outside a narrow script. An Auto-Bot is different: it's trained on your specific business, your services, your pricing logic, your intake forms, so it can actually resolve what a customer needs instead of routing them in circles.

This also covers document intake: dropping in a contract, a form, or an intake sheet and having the bot read it, extract exactly what your systems need, and enter it automatically, no manual retyping.

This is especially high-leverage for salons & med spas businesses, but it applies to any small business fielding repetitive calls or requests. See the full list of AI automation services we build, or how they apply across every industry we serve.

What it does

  • Answers website chat questions using your real services, pricing, and policies
  • Reads intake forms, contracts, and documents and extracts structured data automatically
  • Handles multi-step tasks (quoting, scheduling, intake) instead of a single canned reply
  • Escalates to a human the moment a request falls outside what it should handle
  • Matches your business's tone instead of sounding like generic AI
  • Feeds extracted data directly into your CRM or spreadsheet

Beyond the standard setup

1

Email triage and drafting

The bot reads incoming email, drafts a reply in your voice, and queues it for a one-click approval instead of a blank inbox waiting for someone to respond.

2

Voice-to-structured-data

A technician calls in and talks through a job report; the bot turns it into a properly formatted entry in your job system, no typing required.

3

Pre-visit intake

Customers or patients fill out intake and insurance information through a conversational form before they ever walk in, cutting front-desk time per visit.

4

Multi-step task completion

Instead of just answering a question, the bot can actually complete the task: reschedule the appointment, update the invoice, and notify the tech, all from one request.
