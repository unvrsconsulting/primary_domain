Review & Reputation Management Automation

Your online reputation shouldn't depend on remembering to ask. This system asks at the right moment, and catches problems before they go public.

Positive reviews compound: they drive more local search visibility and more bookings. Most businesses know this but rarely ask consistently, because remembering to send a review request after every job falls through the cracks.

This automation sends review requests automatically at the right moment after a job is completed. It also catches low ratings before they post publicly, flags them internally, and drafts a response so you can address the issue directly with the customer first.

This is especially high-leverage for salons & med spas businesses, but it applies to any small business fielding repetitive calls or requests. See the full list of AI automation services we build, or how they apply across every industry we serve.

What it does

  • Automatically requests a review at the ideal moment after each completed job
  • Sends requests via text or email, whichever gets a better response for your customers
  • Flags a low rating internally the moment it comes in, before it posts publicly
  • Drafts a response for you to review and send
  • Tracks review volume and average rating over time
  • Surfaces what customers are actually saying, good and bad, in one place

1

Cross-platform sentiment monitoring

Tracking mentions across Google, Facebook, and Yelp instead of just one platform, so nothing slips by unnoticed.

2

Competitor benchmarking

A monthly snapshot of how your rating and review volume compare to the two or three competitors you actually lose jobs to.

Auto-drafted responses to every review

Not just an alert on bad reviews: a drafted, personalized response ready for approval on every review, good or bad.

A customer who leaves a 5-star review gets an automatic 'refer a friend' offer while they're still happy, turning your best reviews into new leads.
