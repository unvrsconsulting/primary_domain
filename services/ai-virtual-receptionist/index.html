AI Virtual Receptionist for Small Businesses

A phone system that answers every call like your best front-desk hire would, minus the hold music, the missed calls, and the overseas call center script.

Most small businesses lose somewhere between 60% and 70% of their calls to voicemail during business hours, and worse after hours. An AI virtual receptionist closes that gap by picking up in one or two rings, every time, and handling the call the way a trained employee would.

This isn't the answering service model from a decade ago. A modern AI receptionist is trained on your actual business: your services, your pricing ranges, your service area, your calendar. It answers in a natural voice, understands what the caller actually wants, and either books the appointment directly or captures the lead and texts it to you in real time.

This is especially high-leverage for home cleaning businesses, but it applies to any small business fielding repetitive calls or requests. See the full list of AI automation services we build, or how they apply across every industry we serve.

What it does

  • Answers every inbound call in 1-2 rings, 24/7, with no hold music or transfers
  • Books appointments directly into your calendar (Google Calendar, Calendly, or your CRM)
  • Answers common questions: hours, pricing ranges, service area, availability
  • Captures caller name, number, and reason for calling, then texts or emails you instantly
  • Hands off to a real person immediately for complaints or anything nuanced
  • Sends an automatic call summary after every hangup so nothing gets lost

Beyond the standard setup

1

Bilingual call handling

A second AI agent trained to hold the entire call in Spanish, not just a translated greeting, for businesses with a meaningful Spanish-speaking customer base in the Triangle.

2

Outbound appointment confirmations

The same system that answers calls can also place them: automatic reminder or confirmation calls the day before an appointment, not just a text.

3

Waitlist backfill

When a slot opens from a cancellation, the AI can call down a waitlist automatically instead of that slot sitting empty.

4

Emergency triage after hours

Routine after-hours calls go to voicemail-to-text, but true emergencies (no heat, a burst pipe) get identified and escalated to an on-call number immediately.
