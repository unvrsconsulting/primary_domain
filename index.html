The Problem

A residential cleaning company's best-converting customers were brand-new homeowners: a deep clean is one of the first things people book after a move. But the volume from waiting for leads to come to the business was minimal.

What We Built

A scheduled n8n workflow pulls fresh deed records each day, filters to the service area, scores each homeowner against the ideal-customer profile, and texts the best qualified leads straight to the owner's phone within minutes.
